# 💫 About Me:
Hey! I'm Switt 👋<br><br>
Backend and cloud engineer based in London. I'm happiest deep in distributed systems, where the hard part is never getting it to compile — it's everything that happens at 3am under load.

**What I'm actually good at:**

🧠 &nbsp;**Backend engineering** — APIs, queues, workers, and data models that hold their shape as they grow. Comfortable in TypeScript, Java, Python and Go.<br>
☁️ &nbsp;**AWS & cloud architecture** — I think natively in Lambda, Step Functions, EventBridge, DynamoDB and CDK. Multi-account, infrastructure-as-code, serverless by default.<br>
☸️ &nbsp;**Platform & DevOps** — EKS, autoscaling, observability, and CI/CD pipelines people actually trust.<br>
🤖 &nbsp;**Applied & agentic AI** — multi-agent architectures, tool orchestration, and the unglamorous work of making them safe enough for production.<br>
⛓️ &nbsp;**Real-time & blockchain systems** — high-throughput ingestion, on-chain data, sub-second pipelines.<br>
🧩 &nbsp;**Owning things end to end** — requirements, architecture, delivery, deployment. Usually as the sole developer or the one leading a small team.

I optimise for systems that survive production over systems that demo well. Strong opinions on observability, loosely held opinions on everything else.
